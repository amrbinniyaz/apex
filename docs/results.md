# Results & Achievements

The `/results` page presents the school-supplied CBSE Class X AISSE 2025–26 results. It is linked from the Our school menu, footer and sitemap. The homepage has no new results section or gallery.

The posters were rendered at 3240 × 2160. The class photograph is extracted from PDF image object 94, with the printed heading cropped off at y=203; no pupils are cropped out of the source image. Portraits are crops of the original blue poster, without AI editing. Originals and crops are in `public/assets/results/`; smaller WebP variants are generated by `npm run images`.

Names and percentages are transcribed in `lib/results.ts`. “Mathematics” and “Information Technology” expand the poster labels “Maths” and “IT”. The site uses “Perfect subject scores” without asserting a national ranking. The green poster's “75%–90%” label includes two 74.8% results, so the web caption is “Student results”; the downloadable PDF is unchanged. No old contact details from the posters replace the site's shared school contact information.

To update a year, verify new school documents, update page metadata and copy, replace or add source assets, and update `lib/results.ts` and the results album in `lib/gallery.ts`. Preserve the matching original PDFs for reference. Do not infer cohort size or unreported grades from a photograph.
